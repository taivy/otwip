Introduction

We enter the Labyrinth and find a disgruntled creature named Rhinestone and his rag tag group of the lost. Where they are going, what they have planned, we can't say for sure yet.

All we know is that for better or worse, Rhinestone is their designated group leader. Rhinestone is shown to be quiet and frustrated, often not all that welcoming, and very stubborn. While he does seem to be loved by his friends, he also seems to not want much to do with anyone, and can be seen by himself more often than not.

Rhinestone is ruby red creature with blazing red eyes and softer pink accents. He appears to be thin-furred and somewhat prickly. He is often seen in a hunkering sulk with a semi-permanent scowl.

Before Separation

Rhinestone is born in the Colony of creatures to Azurite and Hypericum, along with his brother, Quartz. After Quartz is murdered, Rhinestone gets closer to his half-sister, Indicolite, and often follows her around for support and comfort whenever things go wrong. Olive, his other half-sister, is also close to him... but one day, she wanders off into the Labyrinth, separating from the Colony. Indi and Rhinestone bond even more over this, though he later finds it to be a one-sided attachment.

Coldness and Grief

With so many of her family dying left and right, Indi begins to distance herself, even from Rhinestone. Whenever he goes to her for comfort, she turns away from him. He takes some comfort in her partner Newmoon, but even then he feels strain on the relationship thanks to Indi's rejection.

Indi comforts Rhinestone.

He starts to keep to himself, only really talking to Newmoon and his childhood friend, Blackberry, who tries to pull him out of his shell multiple times but continues to only be mildly successful.

As time goes on, Rhinestone becomes interested in finding out who the killers are. He has concluded there must be two of them, and starts to silently watch the entire camp for clues. He wants to end his sister's paranoia, hoping to bring her back to some semblance of her old self by soothing this mystery and solving it for her.

Sharing his suspicions with Newmoon, Rhinestone explains he's quite certain that Salmon is responsible for at least some of the murders. There is no way all the food is rotten. He suspects Salmon is doing something to it to make those around them die.

Chase Him Down

Rhinestone chases Salmon.

Seeing the Animals of the Labyrinth is not super common, but is common enough that Rhinestone isn't as scared of them as he probably should be. When the Animals attack while Newmoon is in Salmon's cave, Rhinestone follows Salmon and watches him to see what he does. He watches Salmon see Newmoon crawling out of his hole, and immediately he follows him and chases him down. He isn't going to let Salmon get away with it.
